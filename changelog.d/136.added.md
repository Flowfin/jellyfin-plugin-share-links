Share Links is on your server for the first time. From the plugin's page in the
dashboard you pick one library item, name the guests you are inviting, and get one
link per guest; creating the share is what creates each guest's account, so there is
nobody to prepare beforehand. A share expires on its own, you can revoke it before it
does, and it can carry a ceiling on the bitrate the guest streams at. There are no
anonymous links: a guest signs in, and a link resolves for the account its share names
and for nobody else. The link and the credential are shown once, because only a keyed
hash of the token is ever written down and nothing here can rebuild either of them
afterwards. `docs/operator-guide.md` walks the whole path from installing to revoking,
and `docs/limits.md` is what to read before you rely on a share.
